## Letter to Parents/Guardians (copy/paste, fill [brackets])

Dear Parent/Guardian,

**What is changing:** [School name] will prepare term report cards with Agent Mark, a system that runs on our own office computer. Your child's names, scores, and reports stay at the school.

**What stays at the school:** all names, scores, comments, and report cards — on our computer, encrypted, backed up (USB + sealed copy). Only our approved teachers and head can open them.

**What the internet company holds:** nothing readable. If a teacher works from home, their phone sends a **sealed envelope** (scrambled) through a small relay service. That service **cannot open it** — it only passes it to our school computer, then **deletes it (within ~30 days, or days after delivery)**. Even if hacked, attackers get only scrambled envelopes, never names or grades.

**What we never do:** sell data, show ads against student records, or share one child's record with another school. Anonymous counts (e.g., "many schools hit the same entry mistake") are only ever counted when **20+ schools** are grouped so no school or child can be picked out — and never in early terms.

**Your rights:** see your child's report before it is locked; ask for correction of any error; ask what data we hold; ask for a printed copy; withdraw consent for optional items (photos on reports) without affecting schooling. Contact: [Exams Officer name/phone/office hours].

Signed: Head __________ Date ______

## FAQs (staff script, 30 seconds each)
- *Can the company see grades?* No — sealed envelopes, no keys, auto-delete. Auditor letter available.
- *What if the school computer breaks?* Sealed backup + proven restore drill; reports re-printed identically.
- *Who approves reports?* Class teacher + head, every first term; locked reports show approvers + hash.
- *Internet down?* Everything still works at school; remote sync waits.
